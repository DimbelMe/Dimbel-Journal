---
title: Shooting Star
---
I feel like I'm chasing a shooting star through life, aimlessly wandering through space, pointlessly wondering if it will ever come back. 

I've spent countless nights dreaming of it, reaching my hands to the skies and wishing to hold it again. 

Maybe it's for the best; every time I see a shining star in the night sky, every time I see that reflection in the ocean, it brings me hope that I will find it once more.
