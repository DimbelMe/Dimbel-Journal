---
title: The First Night
---
It's almost three a.m. I've had gotten a call, crime really doesn't sleep.

The Lieutenant sounded tired, nauseous, even. Like he was lacking oxygen. I couldn't even imagine what caused it. 

As I drove there, I kept looking at the moon, reflected on the calm ocean as the breeze steeled my nerves.

Of course, I should have seen it then, but I was by the urgency of the situation.

When I got to the scene, it was already well secured, but the Lieutenant looked pale and tired, more so than usual. And as I approached, I could hear him mumble about retirement.

It was a nightmare. One that, to this day, I'm not sure I've awoken from. The victim; A preschool teacher named Albert Bindel, 32. 

The scene; A sort of ritual, drawings and scribbles painted in blood, something akin to a 'summoning circle' in the middle of his living room, and six of his organs left around it. 

Heart, brain, stomach, lungs, intestines, and eyes. Carefully placed in bowls around the circle, the snuffed out candles accompanying the somber scene. The rest of his body left open on top of his dining room table.

We got his security camera recording, but the person that did it was impeccably professional. Not an inch of skin showing, black clothes, not one look at the cameras. And to top it all, not a single sound or trace. 

A ghost. He showed up without breaking in, he slaughtered Mr. Bindel and left the scene like nothing happened. Not a single twitch as he opened him and removed his organs. Not a single trail to follow. 

I can still see it. The scene, the smell, the taste of blood in the air and that horrible lettering plastered on the walls spelling my name in the victim's blood. 

Two clues. Two empty hints. His silent vendetta, and his professionalism, still makes me shudder at night.
