---
title: An Old One
---
Quiet, unchanging, stoic as stone. A permanent fixture of this land, and of my memory.

The old man sits by the pier. 

Sometimes, as I walk by him, I hear him humming or mumbling the rhythms of old songs, long forgotten by us around him.

'The old one will rise, he will. The old one will come back, he will. The old one will guide us, guide us.'

Only to fall asleep once again, on his broken down chair.
